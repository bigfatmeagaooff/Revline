# Deleting your Revline account and data

**App:** Revline &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Developer:** Revline
**Last updated:** 2026-09-02

This page explains how to request deletion of your Revline account and the data
associated with it.

## Delete your account from inside the app

1. Open **Revline** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll to **Account** and tap **Delete account**.
4. Read the confirmation, tap **Continue**, then **enter your password** to confirm.
5. Your account and its server-side data are deleted immediately.

## If you can't use the app

Email **revline.au@gmail.com** from the address on your Revline account, with the
subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete the account
within **30 days**.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is **permanently removed** from the
Revline servers:

- Your account: email address, username, and the car (make / model / year) on your profile
- Your profile photo
- Every drive you uploaded to your account, including the stats shown on the public leaderboard
- Your follows and followers, likes, and comments
- Any meets you created (and their RSVPs)
- Your notifications and any push-notification device registrations

Content you posted that is attached to other users (for example, a comment on
someone else's drive) is removed along with your account.

## What is kept

- **Drive history stored on your own device.** Revline is local-first: drives you
  recorded are saved on your phone and are not deleted by this process. They are
  not on our servers. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Backups and logs.** Routine encrypted server backups are purged on their normal
  rotation, within **[30] days**. Crash and diagnostic reports processed by our
  error-monitoring provider (Sentry) are not linked to your account after deletion
  and expire automatically under that provider's retention period (about 90 days).
