What to expect

How long does testing run?
Google requires at least 12 opted-in testers active for 14 continuous days before a closed test can graduate. You'll get a link to accept on your phone — that's what starts your 14 days.
What does the app collect?
Precise location while a drive is actively being recorded, your account email, the trips and car details you upload, and crash/diagnostic reports. Location tracking stops the moment you end a drive.
Battery impact?
The Play build only tracks while you have a drive actively started — there's no background tracking, so it only draws power while you're actually recording a run.
I already sideload Revline — what changes?
The Play build is signed differently to sideload builds, so switching over needs a one-time uninstall/reinstall, and any drives that never synced will be lost. Sync first if you've got local-only runs you care about.

Revline is a drive log, not a dare. Record on public roads within the limit, or take the timed stuff to a closed course or track day. The G-force graph is more fun when nobody's calling the cops.
